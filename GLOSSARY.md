# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### Purchasing

**Refund**:
Money given back to a learner for a course they bought. Always the amount the learner actually paid, never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation, Cancel, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The period during which a learner may ask for a Refund: from the day of purchase through the 14th calendar day after it.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, Cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may ask for a Refund on a purchase right now: they are inside the Refund Window, have not consumed too much of the course, and the purchase is not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer
